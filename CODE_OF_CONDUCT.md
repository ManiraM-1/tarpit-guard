# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work.

## Our pledge

We want participation in tarpit-guard to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, and
reviews) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer,
[@ManiraM-1](https://github.com/ManiraM-1). All reports are reviewed promptly
and handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove comments,
commits, issues, and other contributions that break this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is harmful.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
correction, warning, temporary ban, then permanent ban, depending on severity.
